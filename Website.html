Pro Tips

Simple habits that can improve your matches.

01

Always Use Cover

Avoid standing in open areas during fights. Use walls, rocks, buildings and terrain as cover.

02

Listen Carefully

Footsteps and gunfire can provide important information about enemy positions.

03

Don't Rush Every Fight

Winning a match is about making good decisions, not simply getting the highest number of kills.

04

Keep Your Crosshair Ready

Keep your crosshair around likely enemy head or upper-body height.

🚗 Vehicle Strategy

Use vehicles intelligently for rotations, positioning and survival.

🚙 Rotation

Vehicles can help you move quickly when the safe zone is far away.

  • Rotate before the zone becomes dangerous.
  • Choose routes with useful cover.
  • Don't drive directly through obvious enemy positions.

🛡️ Vehicle Cover

A vehicle can sometimes provide temporary cover when terrain is limited.

  • Use it carefully as temporary protection.
  • Park strategically when possible.
  • Don't assume a vehicle makes you completely safe.

🏎️ Choosing a Vehicle

Different vehicles can be useful in different situations and terrain.

  • Consider speed.
  • Consider terrain.
  • Consider passenger capacity.
  • Consider how exposed the vehicle is.

🔊 Avoid Unnecessary Noise

Vehicles can reveal your approximate position. Use them when the mobility advantage is worth it.

  • Don't drive around without a reason.
  • Leave the vehicle when you reach a useful position.
  • Think about nearby enemies before stopping.

🗺️ Rotation Planning

Plan where you want to go before entering the vehicle instead of driving randomly.

  • Check the zone first.
  • Choose your destination.
  • Look for possible stopping points.

⚠️ When to Leave

A vehicle is a tool, not something you need to keep using throughout the entire match.

  • Leave when you reach safe cover.
  • Don't park directly beside enemies.
  • Always have a backup plan.

Smart Vehicle Rotation

01 — Check the Zone

Before driving, check where the safe area is and estimate the direction you need to travel.

02 — Choose a Route

Prefer routes that reduce unnecessary exposure and give you options to stop near cover.

03 — Avoid Predictable Roads

Roads are convenient but can make your movement easy for enemies to anticipate.

04 — Stop at a Useful Position

Don't automatically drive all the way into an enemy-controlled area. Stop where you can continue on foot with cover.

05 — Secure the Vehicle

If you may need the vehicle again, think carefully about where you leave or park it.

Aim Training

Build consistent aim by practicing crosshair placement, recoil control, tracking and reaction time.

Aim Fundamentals

Master these skills before trying advanced techniques.

🎯 Crosshair Placement

Keep your crosshair around upper-body or head height while moving around the map.

  • Avoid looking at the ground.
  • Pre-aim common angles.
  • Keep your crosshair ready.

🔫 Recoil Control

Learn how your weapon moves while firing and practice controlling that movement.

  • Start with short bursts.
  • Practice at different distances.
  • Learn individual weapon behavior.

👁️ Tracking

Practice keeping your crosshair on moving targets instead of constantly flicking.

  • Follow targets smoothly.
  • Avoid over-correction.
  • Practice multiple distances.

⚡ Reaction Time

Improve how quickly you respond when an enemy appears unexpectedly.

  • Practice target switching.
  • Keep sensitivity comfortable.
  • Stay calm during fights.

Daily Aim Routine

01 — Warm Up

Spend a few minutes practicing basic movement and aiming before serious matches.

02 — Recoil Practice

Practice controlling recoil with weapons you regularly use.

03 — Tracking Practice

Focus on smoothly following moving targets.

04 — Target Switching

Practice moving between multiple targets while maintaining control.

Movement

Learn how to move intelligently, use cover, peek effectively and reposition.

Movement Fundamentals

🏃 Strafing

Move laterally while fighting to make your movement less predictable.

  • Use controlled movement.
  • Avoid repeating patterns.
  • Keep aim stable.

🧎 Crouching

Crouching can change your profile during fights.

  • Don't crouch constantly.
  • Use it intentionally.
  • Combine with movement.

👀 Peeking

Use cover to expose as little of yourself as possible.

  • Peek from cover.
  • Return to cover.
  • Avoid standing exposed.

🪨 Cover Movement

Move from one piece of cover to another instead of crossing large exposed areas.

  • Plan your next cover.
  • Use terrain.
  • Avoid unnecessary exposure.

Movement Strategy

01 — Don't Move Randomly

Every movement should have a purpose such as reaching cover, changing position or gaining an angle.

02 — Break Predictable Patterns

Avoid repeating exactly the same movement during every engagement.

03 — Use Cover

Move between useful pieces of cover whenever possible.

04 — Reposition After Fighting

After revealing your position, consider changing your angle.

Map Strategy

Learn landing locations, rotations, zone positioning, terrain usage and final-zone strategy.

Map Guides

🌳 Erangel

A balanced environment with cities, compounds, open fields and varied terrain.

  • Prioritize strong compounds.
  • Watch open-field rotations.
  • Use hills for visibility.
  • Rotate before the zone closes.

🏜️ Miramar

A large desert environment where terrain and long-range positioning matter.

  • Use ridges and hills.
  • Plan vehicle rotations.
  • Avoid unnecessary open-ground fights.
  • Watch long sightlines.

🌴 Sanhok

A smaller environment with dense vegetation and frequent close engagements.

  • Expect close fights.
  • Use trees and rocks.
  • Watch surrounding terrain.
  • Stay aware of nearby enemies.

❄️ Vikendi

A varied environment with towns, open terrain and different elevation levels.

  • Control useful elevation.
  • Use buildings during rotations.
  • Plan vehicle routes.
  • Avoid exposed rotations.

Winning Rotation Strategy

01 — Choose Your Landing Spot

Choose between safer areas for equipment or high-action locations for early fights.

02 — Loot Quickly

Get essential equipment first and avoid spending too much time in one location.

03 — Check the Zone

Check the safe area and plan your next rotation early.

04 — Rotate With Cover

Use buildings, hills, rocks and terrain wherever possible.

05 — Take Useful High Ground

Higher positions can improve visibility, but don't expose yourself simply for elevation.

06 — Prepare for the Final Zone

Keep healing, ammunition and utility available while prioritizing cover and information.

Game Sense

Learn how to read situations, gather information, choose fights and make better decisions.

Game Sense Fundamentals

👂 Sound Awareness

Use footsteps, gunfire and vehicle sounds as information about nearby activity.

  • Pay attention to direction.
  • Notice changes in sounds.
  • Don't ignore distant gunfire.

👀 Information

Gather information before committing to a fight.

  • Check surroundings.
  • Watch enemy movement.
  • Identify available cover.

⚔️ Fight Selection

Not every enemy needs to be engaged immediately.

  • Consider your position.
  • Consider available cover.
  • Avoid unnecessary fights.

🧠 Decision Making

Adapt your decisions to the current situation.

  • Think about the zone.
  • Consider your resources.
  • Adapt to changing situations.

Smart Match Strategy

01 — Gather Information

Check the zone, sounds, terrain and possible enemy positions before moving.

02 — Evaluate the Fight

Consider whether your position, equipment and information give you a reasonable advantage.

03 — Know When to Disengage

If a fight becomes unfavorable, reposition instead of continuing automatically.

04 — Think About the Next Zone

Don't focus only on the current fight. Keep future positioning in mind.

05 — Stay Calm

Avoid rushed decisions after taking damage or encountering an unexpected enemy.

Weapon Selection Tips

01 — Think About Your Fighting Distance

Choose weapons that complement the distances you expect to fight at.

02 — Balance Your Loadout

A useful loadout should give you options for different engagement ranges.

03 — Learn Your Weapons

Instead of constantly changing weapons, spend time learning the recoil and handling of the weapons you prefer.

04 — Don't Ignore Ammunition

A powerful weapon is not useful if you cannot maintain enough ammunition for your planned fights.
